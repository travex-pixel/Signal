# Surface: Today's Signal (src/app/page.tsx) and the app shell

Scope: Operate surface (a daily working screen), with Read surfaces for the
story view, meeting brief and lessons inheriting the same world.
Visitor: a senior commercial leader, five minutes, morning, phone or desk.
Job: know what matters today, why, which region, what the evidence is,
what to ask. Constraints: fits 1440×900 without scrolling; no fake KPIs;
balanced regions; sources visible.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The reasoning chain is the product. The page refuses the
card-grid-of-summaries and the hero-metric template: one lead, a few
supporting items, the chain of reasoning visible as a chain.

OWN-WORLD: "Spec sheet and sample bag." Primaplas's bars-and-beads
polymer chain becomes the evidence chain; the datasheet's property/value
rhythm sets metadata. Navy shell (#231E4B) on a cool tinted ground
(#F5F6FA) with white reading surfaces; region hues from the brand bars:
Southeast Asia cyan, China coral, USA blue, Middle East yellow, Australia
navy, global slate. Schibsted Grotesk for UI and headlines, Literata for
long-form reading. Watercolour pellet/cargo plates in the region hues,
small and editorial, never banners.

STORY: Within seconds the reader sees the one development that matters,
reads why it matters commercially, sees which region, and takes one
question into the day. Deeper reasoning and evidence are one click away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: compact navy top bar (wordmark, five destinations, refresh
state). Main area: left two-thirds, the lead story: region bead, headline
at 28px, two-sentence summary, "Why it matters" paragraph, and the chain
bar (Event · Evidence · Mechanism · Implication · Options · Meeting) as
beads on a bar, each a link into the story view. Right third: up to three
supporting stories as a numbered list with region beads, then the
cross-regional signal, then "One question for today". Footer line: source
count, last refresh, degraded sources.

FORM: the datasheet and sample bag; candidate 4 of my ordered list;

delegated by the brief's instruction to proceed without avoidable
questions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
