# Design System: SIGNAL

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Spec Sheet and Sample Bag"**

SIGNAL borrows its materials from a polymer distributor's own world: the product datasheet and the sample bag of pellets. Primaplas's bars-and-beads mark becomes the reasoning chain (The news · How sure · How it works · What it means · What to do · In a meeting), a bar with six beads that the reader can walk and that follows her down a story. The datasheet's property/value rhythm sets every piece of metadata: publisher · time · region on one quiet line, the Source & evidence panel as a two-column definition list, inputs and results in a simulator as labelled figures. Colour is pellet colour: six region hues taken from the brand bars, used on beads, bars and chart series, never as wash.

The surface is calm and dense in the way a good sheet is dense. One navy shell at the top; a cool tinted ground; white only where something is a discrete object (a card, a button, a chart plate). Hairlines, not boxes, divide content. The headline is the only large type on a page; everything else is either long-form reading in Literata or a small, firm Schibsted Grotesk label. Watercolour plates (pellets, cargo, islands, scales) appear small beside headlines as editorial marks, never as banners, and are hidden from assistive technology because they carry nothing the text does not.

The build refuses the card-grid-of-summaries and the hero metric. There are no gauges, no scores, no decorative charts; the only charts are a waterfall and a single-series line, drawn in SVG with direct labels and hairline gridlines inside a lesson simulator, with every number labelled illustrative.

**Key Characteristics:**
- The chain is the signature: bars and beads from the Primaplas mark, used as the six-step reasoning chain and as region beads.
- Datasheet rhythm: uppercase 12px labels over values, two-column `dt`/`dd` evidence, lining figures on every number and tabular figures only where a column must align.
- Navy shell with a soft gloss, cool washed ground, one sheet of white paper per view with a far, faint shadow; inside the sheet, depth by hairline and tone.
- Two typefaces with strict jobs: Schibsted Grotesk for UI, headlines and labels; Literata at 17px for reading.
- Region hue is information: cyan SEA, coral China, blue USA, yellow Middle East, navy Australia, slate global.
- Claims wear their kind: Fact, Inference, Hypothesis, Unknown tags in four tints.
- Motion is brief and ease-out (150–220ms), reduced-motion respected, 180ms view transitions between routes.
- Mobile keeps the whole product: a five-item bottom tab bar, one column, the chain still on the lead.

## Colors

A navy-and-paper palette with six pellet hues from the Primaplas site, each split into a raw hue for marks, a darker text-safe tint and a pale 100-tint for backgrounds.

### Primary
- **Primaplas Navy** (`navy`, #231E4B): the shell. Top bar, primary button, "The principle" callout, lesson CTA band, active tab text, the default chain colour, Australia's region hue, the `accent-color` of every native control. `navy-700` (#352F66) is the hover of navy things and of headline links; `navy-800` (#1A1638) the pressed state; `navy-200` (#C9C6DD) is the inactive nav text on the shell, the scrollbar thumb and link underline colour; `navy-100` (#E8E7F1) is the Fact tag tint, the active tab pill and the ghost-button hover.

### Secondary
- **Coral** (`coral`, #EE5F5B): China's hue, the active-destination underline in the top bar (3px), the wordmark's outer bars, negative deltas in the waterfall, the marker point on line charts. Text in coral uses `coral-text` (#BF3F3B), which is also `danger`; `coral-100` (#FDE8E7) backs error states in the knowledge check.
- **Cyan** (`cyan`, #40C2D5): Southeast Asia's hue, the wordmark's middle bar, positive deltas in the waterfall. `cyan-text` (#127384) for SEA labels; `cyan-100` (#DDF4F7) for SEA tint.
- **Blue** (`blue`, #2062AF): USA's hue and the focus ring (`outline: 2px solid`). `blue-text` (#1C5699) is the one link colour in the product (inline links, "Open the meeting brief", lesson navigation) and the Inference tag ink; `blue-100` (#DCE7F6) the Inference tint.
- **Yellow** (`yellow`, #FABB14): Middle East's hue. `yellow-text` (#7A5A00) for Middle East labels and Hypothesis tag ink; `yellow-100` (#FEF1C8) is the Hypothesis tint and the text-selection colour.
- **Slate** (`slate`, #8187A3): the global-context hue. `slate-text` (#585E7C) for global labels and Unknown tag ink; `slate-100` (#E9EAF0) the Unknown tint.

### Tertiary
- **Ok** (`ok`, #197A4F / `ok-100`): source counts when every feed answered, "Establishes." leads, a correct answer, the saved notice. Outside the brand palette by intent: state, not region.
- **Warn** (`warn`, #946014 / `warn-100`): degraded-source counts, "Does not establish." leads, the ILLUSTRATIVE VALUES and DEVELOPMENT FIXTURES tags.
- **Danger** (`danger`, #BF3F3B / `danger-100`): no sources reachable, a degraded source row, a wrong answer, a negative simulator figure (via `coral-text`, the same value).

### Neutral
- **Cool Ground** (`ground`, #F4F5F9): the page. Everything sits on it; the story page's sticky chain bar is this at 95% with a backdrop blur.
- **Surface** (`surface`, #FFFFFF): cards, buttons, inputs, chart plates, the bottom tab bar (95%, blurred), bead centres. Not the page.
- **Surface-2** (`surface-2`, #F9F9FC): secondary-button hover, the light band of the skeleton shimmer.
- **Hairline** (`hairline`, #E4E6EF) and **Hairline Strong** (`hairline-strong`, #CFD2E0): all dividers and borders. Strong is for input borders, table header rules, the unwalked chain bar and bead rings, and the dashed empty-state border.
- **Ink** (`ink`, #1C1A33): headlines and reading text. **Ink-2** (#4A4D66): secondary reading text, summaries, publisher names. **Ink-3** (#5C5F82): labels, metadata, chart axes, breadcrumbs, list markers. **Ink-4** (#6B6E90): ordinal numerals, the disclosure chevron, the dotted waterfall connectors.

### Named Rules
**The Raw Hue Rule.** The six brand hues (`navy`, `coral`, `cyan`, `yellow`, `blue`, `slate`) are reserved for marks, beads, bars, underlines and chart series. Any text set in a hue uses its `-text` tint; any field set in a hue uses its `-100` tint. The only exception is navy, which is dark enough to be text and shell at once.

**The Region Is The Colour Rule.** A region hue appears only where it names that region: a bead, a chain, a region-tab underline, a topic bar, a plate. It is never used because it is pretty. SEA→cyan, China→coral, USA→blue, Middle East→yellow, Australia→navy, global→slate, and the mapping lives in one place (`--region-*` in `globals.css`, `hue` in `regions.ts`).

**The Navy Shell Rule.** Navy is the only large field of colour on any page: the top bar and at most one block per page (the primary button, the principle callout, the lesson CTA). Everything else is ground, surface and hairline.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Schibsted Grotesk (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif), loaded via `next/font` as `--font-ui`
**Body Font:** Literata (with Georgia, serif), loaded via `next/font` with the optical-size axis as `--font-reading`
**Label/Mono Font:** none; lining figures (`.tnum`) on Schibsted Grotesk do the numeric work inline, and `.tabular` adds tabular spacing in aligned columns

**Character:** A firm, slightly wide grotesk doing the labelling and headlining, and a modern serif with real optical sizes doing the reading. The pairing reads like a well-set datasheet with a column of analysis beside it: terse in sans, considered in serif. Weights are almost entirely 600 and 500; bold appears only in the wordmark.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 26px → 32px at md, 1.12, -0.015em, `text-balance`): the lead headline on Today (capped at 24ch) and the h1 of story, lesson and simulator pages. The story page goes to 34px at md; the brief to 30px. Headline links darken to `navy-700` on hover.
- **Headline** (600, 22px → 26px at md, 1.2, -0.01em): the one-sentence lede on index pages (Preferences, Sources, Learn), set under a page-title label.
- **Title** (500, 15px–16px, 1.35, `text-pretty`): item titles in supporting and regional lists, lesson and simulator index entries. A region page's first item goes to 19px.
- **Body** (Literata 400, 17px, 1.6, 68ch): `.prose-signal`, the reading column of story, brief and lesson pages. The Today summary and "Why it matters" paragraphs are Literata at 17px/16.5px/15.5px on a 62ch measure; claims are Literata 16px/1.55; the meeting question is Literata 16px/1.45.
- **Body UI** (400, 14.5px, 1.5, `ink-2`): explanatory sans paragraphs, assumptions, option trade-offs, empty-state copy.
- **Label** (600, 12px, uppercase, 0.07em, `ink-3`): every section heading inside a working surface ("Why it matters", "Also today", "Inputs", "Assumptions", simulator stat labels, `dt` in the meeting contribution). Inside `.prose-signal`, `h2` is this label at 13px/0.04em with 36px above and 12px below.
- **Page title** (600, 13px, uppercase, 0.08em, `ink-3`): the `h1` of Today, Preferences, Sources and Learn; on Today it carries the date in `ink-4`, medium, normal case.
- **Control** (600, 13.5px; 14px on phones): buttons, inline action links with an arrow, lesson prev/next, source table body. The top-bar nav is the same size at 500; the mobile tab labels are 11.5px at 500.
- **Meta** (400, 12.5px, `ink-3`): the datasheet line under headlines (publisher · time · beads), breadcrumbs, input hints. Region bead labels are 12px/600; the evidence line under a lead headline is 13px, its confidence label 600 in the state colour.
- **Tag** (600, 11px, uppercase, 0.05em, 1px 6px, 4px radius, always the UI face even inside the reading column): claim-kind tags and status tags. 11px is the floor for any text in the product.
- **Figure** (600, 30px → 36px, 1.1, -0.01em, lining): the lead result in a simulator; secondary results are 17px/1.3.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Literata is used only for prose a person reads through: summaries, "Why it matters", claims, mechanisms, lessons, the question of the day, the original headline quoted in the evidence panel. Everything that is scanned (labels, metadata, controls, tables, charts, numbers) is Schibsted Grotesk. Never set a label in serif or a paragraph of analysis in sans.

**The One Big Type Rule.** The headline is the only large type on a page. Section structure is carried by 12px uppercase labels and hairlines, not by a ramp of heading sizes. A page has one display and never a second.

**The Lining Figures Rule.** Every number carries `.tnum` (lining figures, proportional). Only numbers that must align in a column (the Sources table) add `.tabular`: Schibsted Grotesk pads punctuation to figure width under tabular-nums, so inline times and money (“3:33 pm”, “$1,538”) stay proportional.

## Layout

The page is a 1280px container with 16px gutters on phones and 32px from md (768px). The top bar is 56px of navy with wordmark left, five destinations centred (Today, Past days, Regions, Briefs, Learn), Preferences right; on phones the destinations move to a 56px bottom tab bar (`position: fixed`, safe-area padded) and `main` takes 80px of bottom padding to clear it. Breakpoints are Tailwind's defaults: sm 640px, md 768px, lg 1024px.

Working pages are two columns from lg: a fluid reading column and a fixed aside separated by a 64px gap and a left hairline with 40px padding. The aside width is the one value that moves with the page: 360px on Today, 320px on a region, 300px on a story, 280px on a lesson. Simulators invert it: inputs in a 340px column on the left, results fluid on the right, 48px gap. The meeting brief is a single 760px column because it prints.

The reading measure is 68ch in `.prose-signal` and 62ch for the serif paragraphs on Today and region pages; sans ledes sit at 36–60ch. Vertical rhythm uses Tailwind's 4px scale: 8–12px between a label and its value, 16–24px between sections (section labels sit 24px below the previous block, or 16px plus a hairline in the aside), 32px above a major block, 40px above a page-ending nav. Lists are `divide-y` hairlines with 12–14px of padding per row and never cards.

Today is built to fit 1440×900 without scrolling. A dateline sits above the sheet ("Today's Signal · Friday 30 October", with "Updated 9:41 pm" at the right). Lead left: bead line, headline, the evidence line, summary when the feed has one, "Why it matters" in one paragraph, "In a meeting, you could say" as one quoted Literata sentence, the chain, three actions, and a one-line "The idea behind it" with its lesson. Aside right: three supporting stories numbered (publisher and "and N more" when other outlets reported it), "Across regions", "One question for today". Source health lives on the Sources page, reached from the footer, Preferences and every story's evidence; Today carries no status line. On the story page the chain becomes a sticky bar under the headline (`top: 0`, surface at 95% with blur, hairline below) that fills the step being read, and each section has `scroll-margin-top: 7rem`; a client effect brings a `#section` link to its target after the content streams in and opens the source details when the link names them.

Every tap target is at least 44px tall on phones: buttons grow from 36px to 44px below sm, inline action links carry `min-h-11`, region tabs become a 3 by 2 grid of 44px pills, and the day buttons on a past day are 40px pills. Pages other than Today end with a quiet footer (where the news comes from, Preferences).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is soft and physical. The page is a cool wash (`#F8F8FC` fading to `#EEEFF6`, fixed), and the reading content sits on one sheet of white paper per view: `.paper` is surface, a 16px radius, a hairline of white and `shadow-card`, a navy-tinted shadow that falls far and faint so the sheet reads as resting on the desk, not hovering. Within the sheet, hairlines still divide; nothing nests a second card inside the paper except the quiet `surface-2` "Business relevance" note. Navy fields (top bar, welcome, principle, primary button) carry a gentle vertical gloss (`navy-700` to `navy` to `navy-800`) with a one-pixel inner highlight, so they read as lacquer rather than flat fill. The sticky chain bar and the mobile tab bar use translucency and `backdrop-blur`, the tab bar with a faint upward shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **shadow-1** (`0 1px 2px rgba(35,30,75,0.06), 0 1px 1px rgba(35,30,75,0.03)`): secondary buttons at rest.
- **shadow-2** (`0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35,30,75,0.10), 0 12px 28px -16px rgba(35,30,75,0.28)`): secondary buttons on hover, the focused skip link.
- **shadow-card** (`0 1px 1px rgba(35,30,75,0.03), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(35,30,75,0.07), 0 20px 48px -28px rgba(35,30,75,0.22)`): the paper sheet.
- **shadow-float** (`0 4px 12px -4px rgba(35,30,75,0.18), 0 28px 60px -30px rgba(35,30,75,0.38)`): glossy navy objects (welcome strip, principle callout).
- **Primary button**: gloss gradient plus an inner highlight and a tight navy shadow that deepens on hover; it drops one pixel when pressed.

### Named Rules
**The One Sheet Rule.** Each view has one sheet of paper. Inside it, containers are hairlines and `divide-y`, never nested cards. Shadows belong to the sheet, to buttons and to glossy navy objects; a list never casts one.

## Shapes

Corners are small and consistent: 4px (`sm`) on tags and the focus ring, 8px (`md`) on buttons, inputs, nav pills, the business-relevance box and the knowledge-check options, 12px (`lg`) on cards, callouts, the chart plate and empty states, and full rounds on beads, the chain bar's ends, the tab-bar icon pill and the topic bars. Borders are 1px hairlines; the empty state alone uses a dashed `hairline-strong` border to say "nothing is here". The chain bar is 3px tall with 2px rounded ends; beads are 13px rings with a 2px stroke that fill when active; region beads are 10px (8px small) solid dots. Illustration plates are 4:3 PNGs with transparent grounds, so their watercolour edges are the only soft silhouettes in the system. Geometry is otherwise rectilinear and rules-based: the datasheet, not the dashboard.

## Components

### Buttons
Compact, firm, text-first. Three variants share one frame: 36px tall, 14px side padding, 13.5px/600 Schibsted, 8px radius, 6px gap to an inline arrow icon, `transition-colors 150ms`, 50% opacity when disabled.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px)
- **Primary:** `navy` on `surface` text with `shadow-1`; hover `navy-700`, active `navy-800`. One per view ("Read the reasoning", "Open the meeting brief", "Save preferences", "Check answer").
- **Secondary:** `surface` with a `hairline-strong` border and `navy` text; hover border `navy-200` and fill `surface-2`; active `navy-100`.
- **Ghost:** `navy` text, no border; hover `navy-100`; active `navy-200` at 60%. Used for tertiary actions (Source & evidence, Reset to defaults, Print, Restore defaults).
- **Focus:** the global ring, `2px solid blue` at 2px offset, 4px radius; on the navy shell the ring is white.
- **Text links:** `blue-text`, 13.5px/600 with an arrow, darkening to `navy` on hover; inline prose links are `blue-text` underlined in `navy-200`, underline turning `currentColor` on hover.

### Chips
Claim-kind and status tags are the chip in this system; there are no filter chips.
- **Style:** 11px/600 uppercase 0.05em, 1px 6px padding, 4px radius, a 100-tint field with the matching text tint. Fact `navy-100`/`navy`; Inference `blue-100`/`blue-text`; Hypothesis `yellow-100`/`yellow-text`; Unknown `slate-100`/`slate-text`; Development fixtures and Illustrative values `warn-100`/`warn`; confidence `navy-100`/`navy` at 11px.
- **State:** static; the `title` attribute explains the kind on hover. In a claim list the tag sits in a left margin column on sm and up and inline above the text on phones.
- **Region bead:** a 10px dot in the region's raw hue beside a 12px/600 label in the region's text tint; `RegionBeads` shows up to two (short names) separated by 12px. The bead is the chain's atom and appears in the datasheet line under every headline, in list rows, in region tabs and in Preferences.

### Cards / Containers
Cards are rare: they mark an object that is not part of the reading flow.
- **Corner Style:** 12px (`lg`); 8px for the inline business-relevance box
- **Background:** `surface` on `ground`
- **Shadow Strategy:** only the paper sheet casts one (see The One Sheet Rule); a card inside the sheet is a `surface-2` field with a hairline.
- **Border:** 1px `hairline`; dashed `hairline-strong` for the empty state
- **Internal Padding:** 16px, 20px at md (chart plate); 20px 24px (knowledge check, principle callout); 32px 24px (empty state)
- **Navy callout:** `navy` field, 12px radius, 20px 24px, white 17px/600 title, `navy-200` 12px label, `navy-100` body, a white link underlined in `coral`. Used once per page at most: "The principle" at the end of a story, "Try it with your own numbers" at the end of a lesson.
- **Empty state:** dashed border, 16px/600 title, 14.5px `ink-2` body, one `blue-text` link, max 52ch; it always says what is missing and what to do.

### Inputs / Fields
Simulator inputs are datasheet rows: label left, value right, a slider underneath, rows divided by hairlines.
- **Style:** number field 120px wide, 32px tall, `surface`, 1px `hairline-strong`, 8px radius, 13.5px right-aligned lining text, a 12px `ink-3` unit beside it; range slider 6px tall with `accent-color: navy`; radios and checkboxes also take the navy accent.
- **Focus:** border becomes `navy`; the global blue ring applies to keyboard focus.
- **Segmented control (Preferences):** a `surface` frame with a `hairline-strong` border, 8px radius and 2px padding; options 28px tall, 10px side padding, 12.5px/600 `ink-3`, the checked option filling `navy` with white text on a 5px inner radius.
- **Knowledge-check options:** full-width rows with a 1px `hairline` border and 8px radius, 14px 10px padding; chosen `navy` border on `navy-100` at 60%; right `ok` border on `ok-100`; wrong `danger` border on `danger-100`. The result line leads with "Right." in `ok` or "Not quite." in `danger`.
- **Error / Disabled:** disabled controls drop to 50% opacity and lose pointer events; there is no error styling beyond the knowledge check.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** 56px `navy`; wordmark (three bars and beads in coral and cyan beside SIGNAL at 15px/700/0.14em white) left; five links (Today, Past days, Regions, Briefs, Learn) at 13.5px/500 in `navy-200` with 12px side padding and 8px radius, hovering to white on white at 8%; the current link is white with a 3px `coral` bar under it; Preferences right with a tune icon. Focus rings on the shell are white.
- **Bottom tab bar (phones):** 56px, `surface` at 95% with blur, hairline on top, five equal columns, 20px line icons over 11px/500 labels; the current tab is `navy` with its icon in a 36×24 `navy-100` pill, the rest `ink-3`.
- **Region tabs:** a hairline-ruled row of 13.5px/600 links each led by its region bead; the current tab is `ink` with a 2px underline in the region's hue.
- **Breadcrumbs:** 12.5px `ink-3` separated by a slash, darkening to `ink` on hover.
- **Icons:** one inline set at 24 viewBox, 1.75 stroke, round caps, `currentColor`, 16–20px; nothing is loaded from a library and no icon stands without a label except the Preferences tune icon on phones (which keeps an `sr-only` label).

### The Chain
The signature component. Six equal grid columns; a 3px bar runs from each bead to the next through the bead row; a 13px ring bead sits at the start of each column with the step label beneath (11.5px/600 on phones, where labels wrap to two lines so all six fit without scrolling; 12.5px from md, 12px in the compact story-page variant). Unwalked bars and beads are `hairline-strong`; the chain colour is `navy` by default and the lead story's region hue when a region is given (`--chain-color`). On a reading page, steps before the current one are "done" (bar and ring in the chain colour) and the current bead is filled. Every bead is a link to the matching section of the story (`#event` … `#meeting`), the bead scales 1.3× on hover and the label darkens to `ink`. The same bar-and-bead grammar draws the wordmark, and the region bead is a single bead with its label.

### Evidence line
One datasheet line under every lead headline (Today, a story, a brief, a saved day): a coloured dot and confidence label (High in `ok`, Medium in `navy`, Low in `warn`), then one plain sentence: "Also reported by Bloomberg, The Standard and 2 more." or "No other outlet has reported it yet.", plus "Only the headline is available." when the feed carried no summary. On phones the label and the sentence stack. The other outlets' reports are listed as links in "How sure we are", which is the corroboration itself.

### Datasheet (Source & evidence)
A native `details` disclosure (`#sources`) inside "How sure we are", with a 13.5px/600 sentence summary and the inline chevron icon turning when open. Long redirect links are shown as "news.google.com (redirects to the article)". Inside, a definition list in two columns (`minmax(110px,160px) 1fr`, 24px column gap, 12px row gap, 14px/1.5): property in `ink-3` medium on the left, value in `ink` on the right, lists as bulleted `ul`, the original headline quoted in Literata, the URL as a `blue-text` link with an external icon. The same property/value grammar sets the meeting contribution (`dt` label over `dd` value) and the simulator results.

### Charts
Two restrained SVG charts, each `role="img"` with a title and a 12px caption beneath on a chart plate (surface card, 16–20px padding).
- **Waterfall:** totals in `navy`, negative deltas in `coral`, positive deltas in `cyan` (90% opacity), bars 2px-rounded and at most 64px wide, a `hairline-strong` baseline, dotted `ink-4` connectors between steps, direct 13px/600 value labels above every bar with a leading plus on positive deltas, 12.5px `ink-3` category labels below. No legend.
- **Line:** a single 2.25px line in a brand hue (default `navy`), four `hairline` gridlines with a `hairline-strong` baseline, 12.5px `ink-3` lining axis labels, axis titles in the corners, and an optional `coral` marker (5px ring, dashed drop line, 13px/600 label in `coral-text`).
- Figures are formatted by the caller; the chart knows nothing about money.

### Illustration plates
Procedural watercolour PNGs (720×540) generated by `scripts/illustrate.py`, each with a `.png.json` sidecar recording generator, method, prompt and size. Fifteen plates: six regions, two pellet bags, six lesson subjects, one chain. Placed through `Plate` with empty alt, `pointer-events: none`, `select-none`, and sized small: 128px beside the Today headline, 140px on a region page, 120px on lessons and simulators, 200px on the Learn index, 72px in lesson lists (90% opacity, 100% on hover). Plates are hidden below sm or md and never stretched into banners.

### Motion
`--t-fast` 150ms and `--t-base` 220ms on `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Colour transitions on links, buttons, nav and beads run 150ms; `.rise` fades and lifts a block 6px over 220ms; `.skeleton` shimmers 1.4s linear across `hairline`/`surface-2`; route changes use the View Transitions API (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`) at 180ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every animation and transition collapses to 0.01ms and `scroll-behavior` is auto.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put the chain on any surface that reasons about a story, and link every bead to its section; the chain colour is the lead region's hue.
- **Do** set every piece of metadata in datasheet rhythm: a 12px/600 uppercase `ink-3` label (0.07em) over its value, or `dt`/`dd` in two columns.
- **Do** use Literata at 17px/1.6 on a 68ch measure for anything a person reads through, and Schibsted Grotesk for everything scanned.
- **Do** colour a region only with its hue (`--region-<id>`, `-text`, `-100`) and only where the colour names the region.
- **Do** tag every claim with its kind using the four tints (navy-100, blue-100, yellow-100, slate-100) and keep the legend "How to read this" beside long reasoning.
- **Do** divide lists with hairlines and 12–14px rows; reserve cards (surface, hairline, 12px radius) for objects outside the reading flow.
- **Do** keep one primary button per view, 36px tall, navy with `shadow-1`, and use ghost for tertiary actions.
- **Do** draw charts in SVG with direct labels, hairline gridlines, brand-hue series and a caption, and label every illustrative figure as such.
- **Do** place a watercolour plate small beside a headline (72–200px), `alt=""`, with its `.png.json` provenance sidecar committed.
- **Do** write honest empty and degraded states (what is missing, what to do) in the empty-state container, and show how much stands behind a lead story with the evidence line.
- **Do** keep transitions at 150ms (colour) and 220ms (entrance) on `--ease-out`, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** build a card grid of summaries or a hero metric; Today is one lead, three supporting items and the chain.
- **Don't** use a raw brand hue for running text or as a background wash; use the `-text` and `-100` tints.
- **Don't** add a second large type size to a page; section structure is labels and hairlines.
- **Don't** invent gauges, scores, KPIs, sparklines or decorative charts; the only charts are the waterfall and the line, inside simulators.
- **Don't** cast shadows on lists, inputs or anything inside the sheet; shadows belong to the paper sheet, buttons and glossy navy objects.
- **Don't** load an icon library or an icon font; icons are the inline 1.75-stroke set in `shell/icons.tsx`.
- **Don't** let a plate become a banner, a hero or a background, and never ship a raster without its provenance sidecar.
- **Don't** put more than one navy block in the content area of a page.
- **Don't** render a number in a column, chart or metadata line without `.tnum`.
